Healthcare Operations & Billing Analytics Dashboard

The Problem

A multi-facility healthcare provider had no consolidated view of billing performance, patient volume, or care efficiency — administrators were relying on manual spreadsheet reporting across hospitals.

The Process

Built a star-schema data model in Power BI connecting patient, hospital, and admissions data, then wrote custom DAX measures — including an iterator-based Average Length of Stay calculation — to surface metrics not available out of the box.

Key Insight

Billing and admission patterns varied sharply by admission type and age category — revealing cost concentrations that weren't visible in flat spreadsheet exports.

The Outcome

Administrators can now filter by medical condition, medication, test result, or admission type to drill into specific patient cohorts — without touching raw data.

Car Sales Performance Dashboard

The Problem

A 24K-unit dealership sales dataset needed to be turned into a clear view of where revenue was concentrated — by region, dealer, and vehicle attributes — to guide inventory and marketing decisions.

The Process

Used a treemap to break down average car value by body style and color, paired with a combo chart separating unit volume from revenue trends month over month.

Key Insight

Austin and Janesville led all dealer regions by total revenue, while sales volume showed clear seasonal spikes in September and November–December.

The Outcome

Sales leadership gained a dashboard they can filter by make or individual dealership to spot pricing patterns and seasonal demand at a glance.

Superstore Shipping & Delivery Performance

The Problem

Rather than build generic sales KPIs, this project was framed around one specific question: how does shipping method vary by customer segment and region, and where are delays concentrated?

The Process

Built 100% stacked bar charts comparing ship mode distribution across segments and regions, plus a quarterly trend line tracking shipping mode usage across the year.

Key Insight

Central region had the slowest average shipping duration at 4.1 days against a 3.88-day overall average — and Standard Class shipping dominated regardless of customer segment.

The Outcome

A logistics team could use this to see exactly where delivery delays cluster and rebalance carrier or warehouse routing accordingly.
